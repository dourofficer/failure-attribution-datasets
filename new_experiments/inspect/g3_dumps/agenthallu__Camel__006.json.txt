The droplets on the rough surface are now effectively in contact with 47% coating and 53% air. What would the measured water and oil contact angles of the rough surface be? 
GROUND_TRUTH: Water = 144°, Oil = 125°
FINAL: To calculate the expected contact angles for water and oil on the rough (Cassie-Baxter) surface, we use the Cassie-Baxter equation:

cos θ_CB = f₁·cos θ₁ + f₂·cos θ₂

Where:
θ_CB = apparent contact angle on rough surface
f₁ = fraction of surface in contact with liquid (coating, 47% = 0.47)
f₂ = fraction in contact with air (53% = 0.53)
θ₁ = contact angle of water or oil on the smooth coating
θ₂ = contact angle of water or oil on air (180°)
cos(180°) = -1

For water (θ₁ = 127°):
cos(127°) = -0.6018150232
cos(180°) = -1

cos θ_CB(water) = 0.47*(-0.6018150232) + 0.53*(-1)
